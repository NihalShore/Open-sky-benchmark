From query to evidence

Provenance

Keep archive products, synthetic previews and unverified candidates clearly distinct.

STEP 01

Locate the field

Query by ICRS coordinates and search radius. Record the selected archive collection and request parameters.

STEP 02

Inspect the spectrum

Move through the 102-channel reference grid and inspect wavelength metadata before comparing any measurements.

STEP 03

Compare with care

Use coverage-aware differences as investigation candidates. Confirm calibration, WCS and source association before interpretation.
